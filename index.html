OwlKeep

Privacy Policy

Effective June 2026  ·  OwlKeep LLC

OVERVIEW

OwlKeep is built on a single principle: your private information belongs to you alone. No data from your vault is ever sent to any server, collected by OwlKeep, or shared with any third party, because none of that is technically possible by design.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. OwlKeep has no user accounts, no login, no email address, no analytics, no crash reporting that transmits data off your device, and no telemetry of any kind. There is no server that could receive your data even if we wanted to collect it.

YOUR VAULT DATA

Everything stored in OwlKeep (passwords, documents, photos, financial records, medical information) lives exclusively on your device. It is encrypted using keys derived from your passphrase and bound to your device's hardware. No backup is automatically sent to any server. No sync service exists. If you lose your device without a local backup, your data cannot be recovered by OwlKeep because we have never had a copy of it.

BIOMETRIC DATA

Face ID authentication is handled entirely by Apple's operating system and Secure Enclave hardware. OwlKeep never receives, sees, or stores your biometric data. Apple's APIs return only a success or failure result to the app. Your face data never leaves Apple's secure hardware.

SHARE EXTENSION

When you share a file into OwlKeep from another app, iOS delivers it through an App Group container, a sandboxed folder on your device accessible only to OwlKeep and its share extension. The file is held there temporarily while OwlKeep imports it into your encrypted vault. The temporary copy is removed after import. No file data leaves your device at any point during this process.

IN-APP PURCHASES

OwlKeep is free to download. Full vault access is available as a one-time purchase of $2.99, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. OwlKeep does not receive your payment information, Apple ID, or any data from the transaction. Apple's Privacy Policy at apple.com/legal/privacy governs App Store purchases.

NO ANALYTICS OR TRACKING

OwlKeep contains no analytics libraries, no advertising networks, no third-party SDKs that collect usage data, and no tracking of any kind. We cannot see how you use the app, how often you open it, or what you store in it.

THIRD PARTIES

OwlKeep does not share data with any third party because it does not have any data to share. The only external service involved in OwlKeep's operation is Apple's App Store, used solely for optional in-app purchases.

CHILDREN

OwlKeep does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Because OwlKeep collects no personal information from any user, no special handling is required.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at the same URL with an updated effective date. Because OwlKeep collects no data, any changes are expected to be clarifications rather than expansions of data practices.

CONTACT
